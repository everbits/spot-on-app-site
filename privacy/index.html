SpotOn Energy Privacy Policy

Last updated August 23, 2026. This policy explains how Everbit AB processes personal data when you use the SpotOn Energy app, contact support, or otherwise interact with our service.

Publisher Everbit AB
Privacy contact spoton@everbit.se

Who we are

Everbit AB is the controller of personal data processed through SpotOn Energy for the purposes described in this policy. If you have questions about privacy or want to exercise your rights, you can contact us at spoton@everbit.se.

What information we process

Depending on how you use SpotOn Energy, we may process account and contact details such as your name, email address, and login information; app and device information such as app version, operating system, device type, IP address, and diagnostic data; support correspondence and attachments that you send us; and service data needed to operate the app, such as connected devices, schedules, settings, and usage events generated inside the app.

If you do not use a particular feature, the related category of data may not be processed.

Why we use personal data

We use personal data to provide and operate SpotOn Energy, maintain account access, sync and manage app features, respond to support requests, troubleshoot and fix technical problems, improve reliability and security, communicate important service information, and comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell personal data.

Legal bases

We process personal data when it is necessary to perform our contract with you, such as providing the SpotOn Energy service and core app functionality. We also process data where it is necessary for our legitimate interests, including securing the service, preventing misuse, supporting users, and improving the app. In some cases, we process data to comply with legal obligations, such as accounting or regulatory requirements.

Where a feature relies on consent, you can withdraw that consent at any time, although this will not affect processing that already took place before withdrawal.

Sharing of personal data

We may share personal data with service providers that help us operate SpotOn Energy, such as hosting and infrastructure providers, authentication providers, customer support tools, payment providers if purchases are offered, and providers of diagnostics or crash reporting tools where used. These providers may only process personal data on our instructions and for the services they provide to us.

We may also share information if required by law, to protect our rights, or as part of a corporate transaction such as a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets.

International transfers

If personal data is transferred outside the EU or EEA, we will take appropriate steps to ensure that the data remains protected, such as relying on an adequacy decision or approved contractual safeguards where required.

Data retention

We keep personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy. In practice, this means we generally retain account and service data while your account is active, support correspondence for as long as needed to handle the matter and maintain reasonable records, and certain billing, accounting, or security records for longer where required by law or for legitimate business needs.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to request access to your personal data, request correction of inaccurate information, request deletion of your data, object to certain processing, request restriction of processing, and request data portability where applicable. To exercise your rights, contact us at spoton@everbit.se.

Complaints

If you believe that your personal data has been handled improperly, you can contact us first so we can try to resolve the issue. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Sweden, this is the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY). More information is available at imy.se/en.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in the app, our practices, or legal requirements. The latest version will always be published on this page, together with the effective date shown above.
